Privacy policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

River Guide is a personal paddling map app for Android and the web. It has no accounts of its own, no servers, no analytics and no ads. This page explains what happens to your information.

Data you create

Points, routes, GPS tracks, photos and settings are stored on your device (in the app's own storage, or your browser's storage for the web version). They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your device's location to show where you are on the map, to record GPS tracks you start, and to place photos. Location is used on your device only and is saved only as part of tracks and points you create.

Photos

Photos you import, or take during a photo session, are copied into the app's storage on your device so they can be turned into points. Reading their location tags requires the photo-location permission, which is used only for this.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app stores your points, routes, tracks and photos in a "River Guide" folder in your own Google Drive, so your devices (and people you share a trip folder with) can stay in sync. The app uses Google's limited drive.file permission: it can only see and change files it created itself, or files and folders you explicitly choose with it. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.

The data goes directly between your device and Google. The developer never receives it.

Google Photos import (optional)

If you choose From Google Photos, the app can access only the photos you select in Google's photo picker. Those photos are downloaded to your device and imported like any other photo. The app cannot see the rest of your library.

Use of Google user data

River Guide's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the sync and import features you turn on. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not transferred to anyone else, and not read by humans.

Other services the app contacts

To show maps and river information, the app downloads public data from these services. Like any website, they receive your IP address and the map area being requested:

  • USGS (topographic map tiles and live river gauge readings)
  • OpenFreeMap (trails, parks and other map features from OpenStreetMap)
  • US Army Corps of Engineers (navigation charts)

Removing your data

  • Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes everything stored on that device.
  • To remove synced data, delete the "River Guide" folder from your Google Drive.
  • To revoke the app's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/connections.

River Guide

A paddling map you can edit: hazards, rapids, access points, parking, campsites and routes, with offline maps for the river corridor.

  • Plan routes on the river and save the surrounding map for use without signal.
  • Add and update points with dated observations as water levels and seasons change.
  • Live USGS river gauges, Army Corps navigation channels, trails and parks.
  • Record GPS tracks in the background and turn photos into points.
  • Optional sync between your devices, and shared trips, through your own Google Drive.

River Guide is a personal, non-commercial project. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Open the web app

On Android, install it from the latest release (or keep it updated with Obtainium). Source code: github.com/oklev/river-guide.

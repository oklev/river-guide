        Downloads USGS topo map tiles along the route so the map works without signal. Your points and routes are always available offline.

        Share route

        Saves the route and everything you've added along it as one file (.riverguide.zip). Open it with "Import file" on another phone or computer. Offline maps aren't included; they can be downloaded again.
